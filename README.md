# 📊 ABC Retail — Executive Sales & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard

> **An interactive Microsoft Excel Executive Dashboard designed to transform retail transaction data into actionable business insights through KPI tracking, profitability analysis, sales trends, regional performance, category revenue analysis, salesperson performance, and interactive business filtering.**

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **ABC Retail Executive Sales & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution developed in **Microsoft Excel** to provide management with a consolidated view of retail sales and profitability performance.

The dashboard brings key business dimensions into a single executive view, allowing users to analyze:

- 💰 Net Sales & Revenue Performance
- 📦 Orders & Units Sold
- 💵 Average Order Value (AOV)
- 📈 Profit & Profit Margin
- 📅 Monthly Sales & Profit Trends
- 🌎 Regional Sales Performance
- 🏷️ Category-wise Revenue
- 👥 Salesperson Performance
- 📊 Regional Revenue Contribution
- 🎛️ Interactive Business Filters

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to create a **centralized, interactive, and decision-focused reporting solution** for retail management.

Instead of manually analyzing transactional data, stakeholders can use the dashboard to quickly answer questions such as:

> **What is our current net sales performance?**

> **How much profit are we generating?**

> **What is our overall profit margin?**

> **Which region contributes the highest sales?**

> **Which product category generates the most revenue?**

> **How are sales and profit changing month over month?**

> **How are individual salespersons contributing to overall sales?**

> **How does regional revenue contribution compare?**

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis

## 1️⃣ 📅 Monthly Sales & Profit Trend

The **Monthly Sales & Profit Trend** visualization compares Net Sales and Profit from **January to August**.

This analysis helps management identify:

- Monthly sales fluctuations
- Profitability trends
- Peak-performing months
- Lower-performing periods
- Relationship between sales and profit

# 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Controls

The dashboard includes multiple **Excel Slicers** that allow users to dynamically filter the analysis.

### Available Filters

- 📅 **Month**
- 👥 **Salesperson**
- 📦 **Order Status**
- 🏷️ **Category**
- 🌎 **Region**

Users can select individual or multiple values to perform focused analysis without modifying the underlying data.
